Medical Disclaimer

What the information on this website is, and what it is not.

Last updated 11 November 2026

General information only

The pages on this website are written to help you understand homeopathic care and the conditions our doctor sees. They are general information. They are not a diagnosis, and they are not personal medical advice.

Not a substitute for your doctor

Homeopathy is a complementary approach. It does not replace emergency care, hospital treatment, tests, or the advice of your regular physician or specialist. Please do not delay or avoid seeking medical advice because of something you read here.

Do not stop prescribed medicine

Never stop, reduce or change a medicine prescribed by your physician, including medicines for diabetes (sugar), blood pressure, asthma, heart or kidney conditions, without speaking to that physician first.

When to seek urgent care

Go to the nearest hospital or contact emergency services straight away if you have:

  • Chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting, or sudden weakness or confusion
  • Severe or sudden pain, such as a severe kidney stone pain, with fever, vomiting, blood in urine, or if you cannot pass urine
  • Very high or very low blood sugar readings, or a very high blood pressure reading with severe headache
  • Heavy bleeding, or any condition you feel is an emergency

No guarantees

Every person responds differently, and we do not promise a cure or a particular result. Patient reviews on this site are individual experiences and do not predict your outcome.

Online consultations

Video consultations are useful for discussion and follow-up, but a doctor cannot examine you in person online. Where an examination, test or scan is needed, we will advise you to arrange it.
